# AI-Assisted Business Insights — W5D5

## Dataset
- Dataset: Indian Retail Sales
- Records: 100,000
- Period: 2019–2023
- Country: India

## Key Findings

1. **Overall performance:** Total sales are approximately INR 250.84 crore, with total profit of INR 37.55 crore.

2. **Regional performance:** East is the leading region for both sales (INR 63.53 crore) and profit (INR 9.51 crore).

3. **Category performance:** Sessional Fruits & Vegetables has the highest category sales. Household Items generates the highest category profit.

4. **Customer segments:** Consumer and Corporate sales are almost equal. Corporate sales are marginally higher.

5. **Outlet performance:** Large outlets have the highest sales, followed very closely by Medium outlets.

6. **Product performance:** Beds, Refrigerators and Butter are among the five products with the highest recorded sales.

7. **Yearly performance:** Sales are relatively stable across 2019–2023, with a small decline in 2023 compared with 2019.

## Business Recommendations

- Study the East region's sales and profit performance to identify practices that could be applied elsewhere.
- Review inventory and demand for high-performing product categories.
- Compare Consumer and Corporate purchasing patterns before choosing segment-specific marketing strategies.
- Investigate the small decline in yearly sales and monitor future performance.
- Evaluate product-level profitability before increasing inventory.

## Data Limitations

- Every Customer ID is unique, so repeat-customer behaviour cannot be established from these records.
- All recorded profits are positive; loss-making transactions are not represented.
- Sales Date and Order Date differ in some records. Date-based analysis should use a clearly selected date field.
- These findings describe associations and patterns, not proven causes.

## AI Tool Usage

ChatGPT was used to interpret the generated statistical summaries and develop business recommendations. Python and Pandas performed the calculations. No external LLM API was connected to the application.
