# Executive Summary — Superstore Capstone

## Business Question
How can the business improve sales and profitability across regions and product categories?

## Analysis
The Superstore dataset contains 9,994 order-level records and 21 columns. The analysis examined sales, profit, product categories, subcategories, regional performance, monthly sales trends, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

## Key Findings
- Total sales were 2,297,200.86.
- Total profit was 286,397.02.
- Technology generated the highest category sales (836,154.03) and profit (145,454.95).
- Furniture generated 741,999.80 in sales but only 18,451.27 in profit.
- The East region recorded the highest profit (91,522.78), while Central recorded the lowest (39,706.36).
- Tables had the largest loss (-17,725.48), followed by Bookcases (-3,472.56) and Supplies (-1,189.10).
- Copiers generated the highest subcategory profit (55,617.82).

## Key Recommendations
1. Review pricing, costs, and discounts for loss-making subcategories, especially Tables and Bookcases.
2. Study the successful sales and profit strategies used in the East and West regions.
3. Focus on profitable Technology products while monitoring margins.
4. Evaluate discounts based on their effect on profit, not only sales.
5. Monitor regional and subcategory performance regularly using the dashboard.

## Conclusion
The analysis shows that high sales do not always result in high profit. Improving pricing, discount management, and product-level performance can help the business make better decisions.
